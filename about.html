Our Story 🌸

We're a small Filipino homeschooling family passionate about making learning joyful, meaningful, and accessible for every child.

🌸

Hi, I'm Ate Mae!

I'm a homeschooling mom of three based in Quezon City, Philippines. After years of searching for quality Filipino-made educational materials, I decided to create my own — and Pinay Homeschooler Shop was born!

Every resource in this shop is designed with love and care, drawing from my experience as both an educator and a parent. I believe every child deserves beautiful, engaging learning materials that actually work.

Our products are created to complement the Department of Education curriculum while being flexible enough for any homeschool style — whether you're following a classical, Charlotte Mason, or eclectic approach.

Thank you for supporting our small Filipino business. Every purchase helps our family continue creating resources for your family! 💕
